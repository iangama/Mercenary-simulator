# Demo Guide

## What This Build Is

`Mercenary Company` is currently presented as an `atlas campaign slice`.

The strongest parts of the demo are:

- map-first decision making
- movement, logistics and deadlines
- landmark discovery and short site operations
- journey incidents during travel
- territorial pressure, rival presence and contract timing

This is not yet a giant content-complete sandbox. The demo should be played as a focused run.

## Recommended Demo Session

Target session length:

- `30-45 minutes`

Recommended order:

1. Read the opening briefing and enter the campaign.
2. Stay on the `Map` screen first.
3. Use `Explore Surroundings` at the current node.
4. Open a discovered `Site Operation`.
5. Pick one route and start travel.
6. Resolve a `Journey Incident` if one appears.
7. Reach a contract node and take one contract.
8. Read the `Chronicle` and the result summary.

## What Testers Should Notice

- Does the map feel like a place rather than a menu?
- Do landmarks and routes create curiosity?
- Do travel incidents make movement more interesting?
- Is it clear why a contract is worth taking or skipping?
- Can the player understand what is urgent without outside explanation?

## Best Tester Prompts

Ask testers these questions after one run:

- What was the first thing that felt valuable on the map?
- Did travel feel tense or just slow?
- Did site operations feel worth opening?
- Did the world feel reactive enough after a contract or route decision?
- What confused you first?

## Current Known Positioning

Describe the build as:

- `pre-alpha demo`
- `systems-forward atlas campaign RPG`
- `map, logistics and consequence first`

Do not describe it as:

- full release
- content-complete RPG
- polished final UI

## Good Demo Outcome

A good first session is one where the player:

- discovers at least one landmark
- opens at least one site operation
- hits at least one travel complication
- completes at least one contract
- leaves understanding that movement and timing matter as much as combat
